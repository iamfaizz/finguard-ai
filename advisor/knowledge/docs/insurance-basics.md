# Insurance basics: term and health cover

## Why insurance matters
Insurance protects you and your family from a large financial loss. It is protection, not an investment.

## Term life insurance
Term insurance pays your family a lump sum if you die during the policy period. It has no maturity payout, so the premium is low for the amount of cover. A common rule of thumb is cover of around ten to fifteen times your yearly income, and people with loans or dependants may need more.

## Health insurance
Health insurance pays for hospital bills. Check the sum insured, the waiting period for pre-existing conditions, room-rent limits, exclusions and co-payment. A family floater covers a family under one sum insured, while individual policies cover each person separately.

## Before buying
Compare policies on cover, exclusions and claim settlement record rather than only on price. Disclose your health history honestly, because hiding facts can lead to a rejected claim. Buy only from IRDAI-registered insurers, and keep your policy documents and premium receipts safe.
