# Inflation and compound interest

## Inflation
Inflation is the rise in prices over time. If prices rise by 6% a year, Rs 100 buys what only about Rs 94 bought a year earlier. Savings that earn less than inflation lose buying power even though the balance grows.

## Compound interest
With compound interest you earn interest on your earlier interest, so money grows faster the longer it stays invested. Starting early matters more than starting big.

## The rule of 72
Divide 72 by the yearly return to estimate how many years it takes to double your money. At 8% a year the money doubles in about 9 years. It is a quick estimate, not an exact figure.

## Putting it together
Keep emergency money in safe, easy-to-access places. For long-term goals, look for options whose expected return beats inflation, and remember that higher expected return usually comes with higher risk.
