# Setting a savings goal

## Make the goal specific
A good goal has an amount and a date, for example Rs 50,000 in 6 months. Vague goals like saving more are hard to track.

## The monthly amount
The monthly saving you need is the remaining amount divided by the months left. For example, Rs 50,000 in 6 months needs Rs 8,333.33 a month if you have saved nothing yet. If you have already saved Rs 10,000, the remaining Rs 40,000 over 6 months needs about Rs 6,666.67 a month.

## Check it against your income
Your forecast surplus is the income you expect minus the spending you expect. If the surplus is smaller than the monthly amount you need, you can cut flexible spending such as food delivery, shopping or entertainment, or extend the date, or lower the target.

## Keep it realistic
Cutting every flexible category to zero is rarely sustainable. Small cuts across several categories are easier to keep up than one big cut. Use a longer deadline when the goal is not reachable in the time you set.
