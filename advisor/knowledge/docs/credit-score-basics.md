# Credit score basics

## What a credit score is
A credit score is a three-digit number that shows how reliably you have repaid loans and credit cards. In India the commonly used scores, such as the CIBIL score, range from 300 to 900. A higher score helps you get loans and better interest rates.

## What a good score looks like
A score above 750 is generally considered good by lenders, but each lender sets its own cut-off.

## What affects your score
Payment history matters most, so pay every EMI and card bill on time. Credit utilisation is the share of your card limit that you use, and keeping it low, commonly advised below about 30%, helps. A long credit history, a healthy mix of loans and cards, and few new loan applications also help.

## Habits that hurt
Missed or late payments, using almost your whole card limit, applying for many loans in a short time, and being a guarantor for a loan that is not repaid.

## Checking your report
You are entitled to a free full credit report once a year from each credit information company. Check it for mistakes and report errors to the bureau. Checking your own score does not lower it.
