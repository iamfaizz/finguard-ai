# Scams: fake KYC, digital arrest and phishing

## Fake KYC and bank messages
Fraudsters send messages or calls saying your account will be blocked unless you update KYC through a link or by sharing an OTP. Banks do not ask for your PIN, password or OTP, and you should open your bank's app or website yourself instead of clicking a link.

## Digital arrest scams
Scammers pose as police, customs or investigation officers, claim you are involved in a crime and say you are under digital arrest on a video call. There is no such thing as digital arrest in Indian law. Real agencies do not demand money to close a case, and they do not hold you on a video call.

## Other common tricks
Loan apps that ask for access to your contacts and photos, fake job offers that ask for a fee, lottery or prize messages, and customer-care numbers found through search results.

## What to do
Stop talking, do not pay and do not share any code. Tell a family member. If money is lost, call your bank at once and report on the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in as quickly as possible.
