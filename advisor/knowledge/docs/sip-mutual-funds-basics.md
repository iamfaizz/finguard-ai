# SIP and mutual fund basics

## What a mutual fund is
A mutual fund pools money from many investors and a professional fund manager invests it in shares, bonds or other assets. In India mutual funds are regulated by SEBI. Returns are not guaranteed and the value can fall as well as rise.

## What a SIP is
A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a way to invest a fixed amount in a mutual fund at regular intervals, usually monthly. Many funds accept small SIP amounts, so you can start with a modest sum. A SIP is a method of investing, not a separate product.

## Rupee cost averaging
Because a SIP buys every month, you get more units when prices are low and fewer when prices are high. This can smooth out the average purchase price over time, but it does not protect you from losses.

## Key terms to check
NAV (Net Asset Value) is the price of one unit. The expense ratio is the yearly fee the fund charges, and a lower fee leaves more for you. Direct plans have a lower expense ratio than regular plans. The riskometer in the scheme documents shows how risky the fund is.

## Before you start
Complete KYC, invest only through SEBI-registered platforms, and match the fund type to your time horizon. Past performance does not predict future returns.
