# UPI safety and common scams

## Golden rules
Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, card details or screen with anyone, including people who claim to be from your bank. Bank staff and UPI apps never ask for your PIN.

## You never need a PIN to receive money
If someone asks you to enter your PIN to receive a refund or prize, it is a scam. Entering a PIN always sends money out.

## Collect-request and refund scams
Fraudsters send a collect request that looks like a payment you are receiving. Read every request carefully and decline anything you did not expect. Be careful with fake customer-care numbers found through search results and with strangers asking you to install screen-sharing apps.

## Before you pay
Check the payee name shown by the app before confirming. Scan QR codes only to pay, never to receive. Use official apps from the app store and keep your phone locked.

## If you are cheated
Call your bank immediately to block the account or card. Report the fraud on the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in as quickly as possible, because early reporting improves the chance of recovery.
