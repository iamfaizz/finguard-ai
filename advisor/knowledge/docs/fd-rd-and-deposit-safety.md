# Fixed deposits, recurring deposits and deposit safety

## Fixed deposit (FD)
You deposit a lump sum with a bank for a fixed period at a fixed interest rate. Breaking the FD early usually means a penalty and a lower rate.

## Recurring deposit (RD)
You deposit a fixed amount every month for a fixed period and earn interest like an FD. It helps build a savings habit when you do not have a lump sum.

## Taxes
Interest from FDs and RDs is generally added to your income and taxed at your slab rate, and banks may deduct tax at source above certain limits. Check the current rules.

## How safe is your money
Bank deposits in India are insured by the DICGC up to Rs 5 lakh per depositor per bank, covering the principal and interest together. If you hold more than that in one bank, spreading it across banks keeps more of it insured. Check the current limit on the DICGC website.

## Choosing
Compare interest rates, the lock-in, premature-withdrawal terms and the safety of the institution. A higher rate offered by a very small or unfamiliar institution deserves extra care.
