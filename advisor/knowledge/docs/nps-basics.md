# National Pension System (NPS) basics

## What NPS is
The National Pension System is a voluntary retirement savings scheme regulated by the PFRDA. You contribute regularly during your working years and build a retirement fund.

## How the money is invested
Your contributions are invested in a mix of equity, corporate bonds and government securities. You can choose your own mix or let the system reduce equity as you grow older. Returns depend on the market and are not guaranteed.

## Accounts
A Tier I account is the main retirement account with restrictions on withdrawals before retirement. A Tier II account is a flexible savings option without those restrictions.

## At retirement
A portion of the money must be used to buy an annuity that gives you a regular pension, and the rest can be taken as a lump sum, subject to current rules.

## Tax benefits
NPS contributions can get tax deductions under certain sections, and some benefits depend on the tax regime you choose. Limits change, so check the current rules on the official website before relying on them.
