# Section 80C tax deduction basics

## What Section 80C is
Section 80C of the Income-tax Act, 1961 lets an individual reduce taxable income by investing in or paying for certain approved items, up to a yearly limit of Rs 1.5 lakh. Newer tax law may use a different section number, so always check the as-of date.

## Old and new tax regime
The deduction is available only if you choose the old tax regime. Under the new tax regime most deductions, including 80C, are not allowed. Compare both regimes before choosing.

## Common eligible options
Employee Provident Fund (EPF) contributions, Public Provident Fund (PPF), Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) mutual funds, life insurance premiums, National Savings Certificate (NSC), five-year tax-saver fixed deposits, Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana, repayment of home-loan principal and children's tuition fees.

## Lock-in periods
ELSS has a three-year lock-in. Tax-saver fixed deposits and NSC have five years. PPF runs for 15 years with limited partial withdrawals. Check the lock-in before investing so your emergency money is not locked away.

## Important note
Tax rules change often. This summary is general education, not tax advice. Confirm current limits on the official Income Tax Department website or with a tax professional.
