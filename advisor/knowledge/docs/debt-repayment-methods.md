# Repaying debt: avalanche and snowball methods

## Start with the basics
List every loan and card with its balance, interest rate and minimum payment. Pay at least the minimum on all of them so you avoid late fees and damage to your credit score.

## Avalanche method
Put every extra rupee on the debt with the highest interest rate, and move to the next highest when it is cleared. This usually costs the least interest overall.

## Snowball method
Put every extra rupee on the debt with the smallest balance first. Clearing small debts quickly can keep you motivated, though you may pay more interest overall.

## Which one to choose
Choose avalanche if you want to save the most money, and snowball if you need early wins to stay on track. Either works better than paying randomly.

## Good practice
Keep a small emergency fund so that a surprise bill does not push you back onto a credit card. Avoid taking a new loan only to repay an old one unless the new rate is clearly lower, and check processing fees and prepayment charges first.
